About Raksha Bandhan

Raksha Bandhan is a vibrant Indian festival that honors the cherished bond between siblings. Sisters tie a sacred thread — the rakhi — on their brothers’ wrists, symbolizing love and protection. In return, brothers offer gifts and a vow to stand by their sisters through all of life’s journeys.

Across homes, sweets are shared, diyas glow warmly, and families come together. The spirit of the day is anchored in gratitude, care, and lifelong friendship.
